## 1\. What goes in the head of an HTML page, and what goes in the body? Give one example of each. The head of the website contains the metadata of the site. For example, a title for the browser. The body section of a website is for content like a paragraph. For example, your main content of the site. In body paragraphs, these can be separated with line breaks.

## 2\. What is the difference between the title element and the heading level one? Where does each one show up? The title of the site shows up in the browser tab. The heading level one is basically the first part of the site and it's basically the headline of the site. For example, a headline of a news source that's the most prominent would be heading level one.

## 3\. Why does a page have only one heading level one, with level twos under it? The main heading which is heading level one indicates the important part of the site like the news headline. But the heading levels below that like heading level two, and the rest of the headings are subsections of that site.

## 4\. What does the doctype line do? The doctype line in Hypertext Markup Language also known as HTML, declares that the document well is indeed HTML.

## 5\. What is an HTML comment for? Who never hears it? A comment in HTML is used, for developers to leave notes in their site. However, the browser does not render this data which that means therefore the person reading the site does not see the comments.
