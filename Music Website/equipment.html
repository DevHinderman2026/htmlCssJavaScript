This is the Equipment page. It describes the digital audio workstations I use for music production. There are two sections below, in this order: GarageBand, then Logic Pro. After the last section you will reach the page footer, which holds my name and my email address.

GarageBand

GarageBand is where I start. I began using it in 2021 and have used it ever since. It has a 32 track count, which means I can record up to 32 instrument and audio tracks inside a single project. It also supports volume automation.

Logic Pro

Logic Pro is my professional digital audio workstation. I take projects from GarageBand, move them into Logic Pro, and then I do my mixing and mastering there. If time allows, I create projects directly in Logic Pro and work on full-scale projects with professional workflows.
