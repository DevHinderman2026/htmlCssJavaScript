Overview of: ai01 Document Structure
Course: Web Design AI track (archived version)

What this lesson is about:
This archived unit is the first real build of the AI track: an About Me page created by directing Claude. It covers the full HTML5 skeleton (doctype, html, head, body), what belongs in the head (title, charset, viewport) versus the body, the difference between the title and the heading level 1, correct heading levels with no skipping, real paragraph elements, and HTML comments. You audit your own page by ear, checking the announced title, the headings outline, and your content, and you deliberately remove the title and skip a heading level to hear the damage. You also have Claude walk through the page line by line so you can explain every line you never typed. The matching lesson in the HTML CSS JavaScript track is html01 Document Structure.

Documents in this folder, in suggested order:
1. ai01 BuildBrief summary.txt — what to direct Claude to build, the must-say vocabulary, the line-by-line twist, and the files to commit.
2. ai01 AudioLab summary.txt — the four-step audit of aboutme.html, including two break-it-on-purpose experiments.
3. ai01 ConceptCheck summary.txt — the topics the ai01 ConceptCheck sheet checks (questions left out; they are homework).
