In one sentence:
This is the planning template you fill in for the 11a mini-proposal; it was adapted from the retired html14 capstone planning template, so wherever it says "capstone," read "Build It Three Ways."

Main ideas:
1. A note at the top says this is version 2, adapted from the old capstone planning template, to be used for the 11a mini-proposal.
2. You complete it before you start coding. Its purpose is to organize your thinking and plan the site's structure first.
3. It has 12 sections plus a teacher notes area. It plans a four-page site: Home, About, Services or Portfolio, and Contact, with a table on the services page and a form on the contact page.
4. Key planning ideas it teaches: a sitemap lists every page and how they connect; a wireframe is a rough box sketch of a page layout made before coding; a color palette of 3 to 5 colors with hex codes; choosing fonts from Google Fonts; writing real content (never Lorem Ipsum placeholder text); planning form validation, responsive behavior, accessibility, and SEO from the start.

Key terms:
Sitemap — a map of all the site's pages and how they link together.
Wireframe — a simple sketch of where the header, content, images, and footer go on a page.
Hex code — a color written as a pound sign followed by six characters, such as #1a2b3c.
Lorem Ipsum — fake Latin filler text; not allowed here.
Title tag — the page title shown in browser tabs and search results.
Meta description — the short page summary that search engines show.
Form validation — checking what the user typed before accepting a form.

What you are asked to do:
Fill in every field below.

Section 1, Project Overview:
- Website name or title.
- Website theme or purpose: what it is about, what problem it solves, who it is for.
- Target audience: who will visit.
- Your goal for the project: what you want to accomplish and are most excited to build.

Section 2, Sitemap (describe each of 4 pages):
- Page 1, Home (index.html): its purpose, and a content outline that includes a navigation menu linking all pages, a hero section with main headline, your other sections (such as intro, featured content, call to action), and a footer with copyright and social links.
- Page 2, About (about.html): its purpose, and a content outline with navigation, heading and introduction, your sections (such as history, team bios, mission statement), a call to action linking to contact or services, and a footer.
- Page 3, Services or Portfolio (services.html or similar): its purpose, and a content outline with navigation, heading and introduction, your sections (services, portfolio items, products), a TABLE (describe what it shows, such as pricing, team list, feature comparison, menu items, or schedule; what goes in each column; how many rows), a call to action, and a footer.
- Page 4, Contact (contact.html): its purpose, and a content outline with navigation, heading and introduction, a FORM (which fields, such as name, email, phone, message; whether it has a submit button and what happens on submit), contact information (email, phone, address, social links), and a footer.

Section 3, Visual Design:
- Color palette of 3 to 5 colors, chosen with a tool such as Coolors.co or Adobe Color: primary color hex code and where you will use it (buttons, headings, header background); secondary color hex code and where (accents, borders, hover states); accent color hex code and where (highlights, alerts, call-to-action buttons); neutral colors: a light background hex code and a dark text hex code.
- Typography from Google Fonts: heading font name, its Google Fonts link, and why (what mood it sets); body text font name, its link, and why.

Section 4, Content Plan:
- Images checklist: logo if applicable, hero or banner image, About section images, Services or Portfolio images, other images such as icons or backgrounds.
- Total images needed (aim for 3 to 4 minimum).
- Where images will come from: your own photos, free stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay), or a placeholder service (picsum.photos).
- Text content checklist: a 1 to 2 sentence home headline, a 2 to 3 paragraph About description, a list of services or products with descriptions, and any other text such as footer text and form labels. No Lorem Ipsum; write real content.

Section 5, Wireframe Sketches (draw on paper or describe in words; simple boxes are fine; show where the header and navigation, main content, images, and footer go):
- Sketch 1, Home: header with nav at top, hero section, content sections, call-to-action button, footer.
- Sketch 2, About: header, heading, about content with images, footer.
- Sketch 3, Services or Portfolio: header, heading, service sections, the table (describe its structure), footer.
- Sketch 4, Contact: header, contact information, the form (describe fields), footer.

Section 6, Form Details:
- Form title or heading.
- Fields, each marked Required yes or no: Name (text input), Email (email input), Phone (tel input), Subject or Reason (select dropdown or text), Message or Comments (textarea), and a Checkbox (such as "I agree to be contacted" or "Subscribe to newsletter").
- Validation rules to check on submit: name not empty; email in valid format; phone is 10 digits (or your format); message at least 10 characters; checkbox must be checked; any other rule you add.
- What happens after submission: an alert box, a message displayed on the page, a page redirect, or an email notification.

Section 7, Table Details:
- Table title.
- Table purpose (what information it shows).
- Column headers: 3 required, plus an optional fourth.
- At least 5 to 6 rows of planned data.

Section 8, Navigation Structure:
- Which navigation you will have: main header menu (Home, About, Services, Contact), footer navigation, breadcrumbs (optional), sidebar menu (optional).
- Navigation style: horizontal menu at top, vertical side menu, hamburger menu on mobile, or other.

Section 9, Responsive Design Strategy:
- Mobile (under 600 pixels): navigation (hamburger, stacked, or hidden), layout (single column, centered), images (full width or smaller), font sizes.
- Tablet (600 to 1024 pixels): how navigation adapts, number of columns, image size.
- Desktop (1024 pixels and up): full horizontal menu or not, multi-column or side-by-side layout, image size.

Section 10, Accessibility Checklist (check each):
- All images have descriptive alt text.
- Heading hierarchy is correct (h1, h2, h3, no skipped levels).
- Form labels are properly connected to their inputs.
- High color contrast.
- Navigation works by keyboard (the Tab key).
- No flashing or rapid animations that could trigger seizures.

Section 11, SEO Basics: for each of the four pages (Home, About, Services, Contact), a title tag of at most 60 characters and a meta description of 100 to 160 characters.

Section 12, Project Timeline (six sessions):
- Session 1, Planning: complete this document.
- Session 2, HTML: all 4 pages created, navigation working, all content added.
- Session 3, CSS: all pages styled and responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Session 4, JavaScript: form validation working and interactive features added.
- Session 5, Testing: validated, tested, bugs fixed.
- Session 6, Deployment: live on GitHub Pages, repository created, self-assessment done.

Teacher Notes (filled by the teacher): student name or names, theme approved by, date, and comments.

Watch out for:
- Do not use Lorem Ipsum.
- Plan accessibility and SEO from the start, not at the end.
