In one sentence:
This is the independent project for lesson 01: build a complete "About Me" page from scratch, with no starter code, and submit it as aboutMe.html in your GitHub folder.

Main ideas:
1. This task pulls together everything from sub-lessons 01a and 01b: the HTML skeleton, headings, paragraphs, line breaks, and comments.
2. You type the whole structure from memory rather than copying and pasting from examples, which is how the skeleton gets memorized.
3. Content should be real and personal. Headings should honestly describe the text under them, and each section needs meaningful writing, not placeholder text like "Lorem ipsum".
4. A suggested outline (you may use your own): an h1 with your name; an h2 "About Me" with a short bio paragraph about what you are passionate about; an h2 "My Interests" with an h3 "Technology" and a paragraph on what tech interests you and why; an h2 "Education and Goals" with a paragraph on what you are studying and want to accomplish; and an h2 "Fun Fact" with something unique about you.

Key terms:
aboutMe.html — the required file name (lowerCamelCase).
meta charset — required tag in the head setting character encoding.
title — must read "About Me".
h1, h2, h3 — heading levels used for your name, sections, and a subsection.
br — line break inside a paragraph.
Comment — developer note in the code; at least three required.

What you are asked to do:
Submit one HTML file named aboutMe.html to your GitHub folder.
Structure requirements:
- Valid HTML5 with the doctype, html, head, and body tags.
- A proper meta charset tag in the head.
- A title tag in the head that reads "About Me".
- Proper indentation and neatly organized code.
Content requirements:
- Exactly one h1 heading containing your name.
- At least three h2 headings for different sections (examples: About Me, My Interests, Goals).
- At least one h3 subheading within a section.
- At least 4 paragraphs total.
- At least one br tag creating a line break inside a paragraph.
- At least 3 comments explaining sections or your thinking.
Writing requirements:
- Genuine content that reflects who you are; meaningful information in every section; headings that match their content; correct grammar and spelling.
How to start:
1. Create a folder named html01_AboutMe.
2. Open it in VS Code.
3. Create aboutMe.html.
4. Type the full structure from memory: doctype, html, head, body, meta charset, and title.
5. Add headings, paragraphs, and comments.
6. Save and test in the browser with Live Server.
7. Review the code for indentation and comments.
Checklist before submitting: file named aboutMe.html; doctype present; all tags properly closed; meta charset in the head; title "About Me" in the head; one h1 with your name; at least three h2s; at least one h3; at least 4 paragraphs with real content; at least one br; at least 3 comments; neat indentation; page displays without errors; all content original and about you.
Grading rubric, 100 points total:
- Valid HTML structure (doctype, html, head, body): 20 points.
- Proper use of headings (h1, h2, h3): 15 points.
- Content quality and originality: 15 points.
- Paragraphs and line breaks: 15 points.
- Comments in code: 10 points.
- Code organization and indentation: 10 points.
- Functionality (displays in browser, no errors): 15 points.
After finishing: review the study guide vocabulary, play the Gimkit review quiz, then take the assessment quiz.

Watch out for:
- Do not leave any tag open; check opening and closing tags and spelling if something does not work.
- The title text must be exactly "About Me".
- Only one h1.
- Get the structure right first, then add content, and test often.
- If stuck, go back to the html01 Walkthrough.
