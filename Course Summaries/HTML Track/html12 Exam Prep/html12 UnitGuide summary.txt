In one sentence:
This unit guide lays out the final exam-prep unit: a required WebXam review lane for everyone and an optional Certiport certification lane, with the day-by-day plan and materials for each.

Main ideas:
Timing: html12 is the last unit of the course, about 8 class days running November 30 to December 9. The WebXam test comes early in the December 10 to 17 window. The Certiport ITS HTML and CSS certification attempt comes later in the same window, with early January as the fallback.

Lane 1, WebXam review, for everyone, about 3 to 4 days:
1. Day 1: mixed review of HTML, CSS and forms using recombined question banks from earlier units and Gimkit rounds, from the unit Gimkit CSV files.
2. Day 2: troubleshooting drills using methodology scenarios for competency 2.11 (top-down, bottom-up, follow-the-path, spot-the-differences), plus a Strand 1 vocabulary pass covering proposal, testing and acceptance, and rollout terms from html11 and business terms. Material: html12_TroubleshootingScenarios.
3. Day 3: full review day using html12_MasterStudyGuide.
4. Day 4: a timed practice exam, 40 questions in 45 minutes, followed by a distractor autopsy of the questions you missed. Material: html12_PracticeExam.

Lane 2, Certiport gap lane, opt-in and ungraded, about 3 to 4 days. It is built on a separate Certiport HTML and CSS study guide (Certiport_HTML_CSS_StudyGuide.md, in a folder called certiportStudyGuidesForGitHub). Students who do not opt in use these days for extra Lane 1 review, polishing their html11 project, or portfolio work.
1. Day 1: content only on the certification exam: image maps (the usemap attribute and the area element), character entities, HTTP GET versus POST, the track element, meta tag details, and display versus visibility versus overflow. Study guide modules 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 12.
2. Day 2: deeper certification content: the specificity ladder and cascade arithmetic, inheritance, the !important rule, and CSS units (relative versus absolute). Modules 9, 10 and 11.
3. Day 3: drills, a distractor autopsy on the guide's mock quiz (worksheet html12_CertLane_DistractorAutopsy.md) and GMetrix practice attempts. Module 13.
4. Day 4: review the cheat card (print html12_CertLane_CheatCard.md) and a final GMetrix pass. Cheat card plus glossary.

Notes from the guide:
- There is no JavaScript on the Certiport exam; Lane 2 is purely precise HTML and CSS.
- The practice exam and study guide were taken from last year's sprint materials and had Bootstrap removed, since Bootstrap is no longer part of the course. If a question seems off-topic, the master plan's coverage table (the course outline) is the authority on what is in scope.

Key terms:
WebXam — the state end-of-course web design exam everyone takes.
Certiport ITS HTML and CSS — an optional industry certification exam (ITS stands for IT Specialist).
GMetrix — practice-test software for the Certiport exam.
Distractor autopsy — reviewing why each wrong answer choice is wrong.
Lane — one of the two parallel study tracks in this unit.
Strand 1 — the part of the course outline covering business and project terms.

What you are asked to do:
Follow Lane 1 (all students). Optionally join Lane 2. Take the 40-question, 45-minute timed practice exam on Lane 1 day 4 and autopsy your misses.

Watch out for:
- Correction: the practice exam is 40 questions in 45 minutes, as this guide says; the practice exam's own "60 minutes" header and its "60 questions in 60 minutes" tip are wrong. The real Certiport IT Specialist HTML and CSS exam is 35 to 45 questions in 50 minutes with 70 percent to pass, so plan for about 50 minutes on that one.
- Heads up: the Certiport study guide (with its 12-question mock quiz) and the master plan's coverage table are not in this lesson folder. Ask Mr. McMaster for the Certiport guide if you join Lane 2; until then, the CheatCard and the master study guide here are what you have.
