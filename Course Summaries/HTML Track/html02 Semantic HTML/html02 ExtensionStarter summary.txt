In one sentence:
This is the intentionally badly coded "Riverside Community Garden" page that you repair in the Div Soup Rescue extension task.

Main ideas:
The page has a correct head (doctype, lang en, meta charset UTF-8, title "Riverside Community Garden") but a body built entirely from div elements, with no comments or TODOs. Its content, top to bottom, and what is wrong with each part:
1. Site title "Riverside Community Garden": a div containing b text with an inline font size of 28 pixels. It is a fake heading and should be the h1, inside a header.
2. Menu "Home | Plots | Events | Contact": nested divs with each word in b. It should be a nav region. The words are not even links.
3. "Welcome to the Garden": b text at 22 pixels in a div. It should be an h2 inside main.
4. A paragraph-like div: the garden has been growing since 2009, and "we think it is the best place in town", with "best" in i. This should be a p, with em on "best".
5. A div: "Membership is open to everyone. No experience required." with the second sentence in b. This should be a p, with strong on "No experience required."
6. "What You Can Grow": b text at 18 pixels. It should be an h3 (or h2).
7. A div of lines separated by br tags: Tomatoes, Peppers, Sunflowers, Pumpkins, Herbs of all kinds. Order does not matter, so this should be a ul with li items.
8. "Rules of the Garden": another 18-pixel b fake heading.
9. A div of three rules separated by br: water your plot at least twice a week; compost goes in the green bin; lock the gate when you leave. This should become a real list.
10. Footer line "Riverside Community Garden - 442 River Rd - Est. 2009" in nested divs. It should be in a footer.

Key terms:
div — a generic container with no meaning.
Inline style — the style attribute (for example style="font-size: 28px;") used here to fake heading sizes.
Fake heading — bold, enlarged text that a screen reader will not announce as a heading.

What you are asked to do:
The file has no instructions inside it. The html02 Extension Task says to copy it to html02_ExtensionRescue_lastname.html and replace each non-semantic piece with the correct element, as outlined above.

Watch out for:
- With a screen reader this page has no headings, landmarks, or lists to jump between; that is the problem you are fixing.
- The list of lines separated by br tags is one block of text, not a list.
