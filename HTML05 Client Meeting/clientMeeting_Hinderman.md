# Client Meeting — Russell Hinderman

## Part 1: Research

### Source 1: GoDaddy

[Website design client questionnaire to understand your clients' needs](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/the-ultimate-web-design-client-questionnaire)

What I learned:

When making a website, it is very important to ask the right questions. For example, tell us about your company. Have you ever had a website? Do you need a new website?

If you ask the wrong questions, you will lose time and money.

### Source 2: Meta Digital

[How to Prepare for a Website Discovery Meeting](https://www.metadigital.co.nz/articles/how-to-prepare-for-a-website-discovery-meeting/)

What I learned:

A website discovery meeting is a way for a web team to know your needs.

Some questions that a team might ask could be the following.

- What products or services do you sell?
- Who is your target audience?
- These are just some examples.

### Source 3: Liquid Web

[Build a Web Design Client Intake Form](https://www.liquidweb.com/blog/how-to-create-a-client-intake-form/)

What I learned:

A web intake form is a lightweight screening tool to help clients see if they are a good fit for you to create a website.

Forms give structure and save you time.

The biggest perk is that it improves client conversion.

## Part 2: Client Questions

### Section 1: Business

Question 1: Tell me about your company.

Question 2: What products or services do you promote or sell?

Question 3: When we start working on your site, what is your goal?

### Section 2: The Audience

Question 4: What would you like your visitors to do when they find your site?

Question 5: What should visitors see first when they find your website?

Question 6: Should visitors have to log in to your site?

Question 7: What parts of your site should be gated by a login?

Question 8: What accessibility needs should your website meet so every visitor can use it, such as people who use screen readers, need larger text, or cannot use a mouse?

### Section 3: The Content

Question 9: What should your home page contain?

Question 10: How would you like your page to look?

Question 11: For the about page, would you like your contact information to be displayed there, or would you like a separate page?

Question 12: How should the navigation menu on your site work? Should it show visitors the main links, or action items they could take on your site, such as shortcuts?

Question 13: Who will write the words for your site?

Question 14: If you have photos or videos on your site, do you have the necessary permissions to use them?

### Section 4: Branding

#### Client who already has branding

Question 15: Are you starting from scratch? Have you made a site before?

Question 16: Tell me about your logo. Where should it go?

#### Client starting from scratch

Question 17: What colors do you think your customers would find appealing when visiting your site?

Question 18: Describe any photos that you would want on your site.

### Section 5: The Look

Question 19: Think of a site you have visited. Name a few things that you liked or disliked about it.

Question 20: Tell me about your preferences for colors throughout your site.

Question 21: Do you think that your site will benefit from adjustable font controls built in?

### Section 6: The Project

Question 22: When would you like the site to be finished?

Question 23: Do you have a domain already?

Question 24: Who will update the site if it needs updates after it is live?
