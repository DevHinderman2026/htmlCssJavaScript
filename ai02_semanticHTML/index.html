Music

The most current album I'm working on is called Paranoia. The way I produce music is I work on big catalogs of albums, and then hand pick which ones I'd like to distribute.

  1. Sketch out ideas and build a large catalog of songs.
  2. Produce the tracks that show the most promise.
  3. Hand pick the strongest songs for the album.
  4. Mix and master the final selections.
  5. Distribute the finished album.
